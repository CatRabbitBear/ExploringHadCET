### Reading the surface view

This view uses the same monthly mean temperature data as the previous chart, but arranges each year along an additional axis. Instead of individual yearly lines, the data forms a continuous surface that shows how the seasonal pattern changes across time.

By viewing the record this way, it becomes easier to see how groups of years relate to one another and where periods of similar behaviour cluster together. Broad features that are difficult to follow across many overlapping lines in the 2D view become more apparent when seen as a surface.

This representation also allows gentle smoothing across neighbouring years. The aim is not to replace the underlying measurements, but to reduce visual noise so that longer-term structure and regions of interest are easier to identify.

Details of how this surface is constructed, and the choices involved in smoothing, are discussed in the Methodology section.
