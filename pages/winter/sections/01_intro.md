This page uses the same temperature data shown earlier, but rearranges the view to focus on winter months. Winter is defined here as **December, January, and February (DJF)**.

For each selected time span, the DJF months are first shown together, overlaid in a single view. By default, results are grouped by century for both the instrumental record and the full series. An animation then separates the months and expands them into boxplots, allowing December, January, and February to be compared side by side.

This progression is designed to make differences in spread and variability between winter months easier to see, while keeping the underlying data and time grouping consistent throughout.
